About Me

A dependable operator with an entrepreneurial mindset

Since 2014, I have run my own retail distribution and general trading business in Owerri, Imo State, serving as an authorized distributor for Indomie products across my local market. What started as a single trading relationship has grown into a business built on consistency, integrity, and strong customer relationships.

My academic foundation in Accounting from the University of Nigeria, Nsukka gives me a disciplined, numbers-driven approach to running a business, from managing stock and cash flow to pricing and supplier negotiations. I pair that structure with a genuine passion for people: understanding what customers need, solving problems on the spot, and adapting quickly as the market changes.
